## Data limitations

Prices are posted-bid observations, not guaranteed bids. Freight, drying, shrink, elevation, premiums, discounts, payment terms, capacity, and contract conditions are excluded unless specifically documented. Confirm the current bid, commodity specification, grade, delivery period, futures contract, currency, unit, and freight treatment before acting.
